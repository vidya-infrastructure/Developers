ABOUT US

Experience across

Vidya Infrastructure & Developers is a construction company based in Solan, Himachal Pradesh, with experience across residential, hospitality, institutional, infrastructure and commercial projects.

Over the years, the company has been associated with a diverse range of construction projects for reputed private organizations and government institutions. Our work has included relationships with clients, architects, engineers, subcontractors and suppliers.
